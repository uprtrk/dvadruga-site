About us

Two friends

We’re not an agency or a company with a call centre. We’re two people who live here, love the sea and take pride in our work.

Our story

How it started

A few years ago we started washing yachts for people we knew — simply because we do it well and enjoy the result. The first clients came by word of mouth, then regulars followed, then people started finding us on their own.

Today we look after yachts across the Bay of Kotor, from Herceg Novi to Tivat. We have no hired staff we don’t personally know. We come ourselves.
