FAQ

Questions about prices

My yacht is over 10 metres. What does it cost?

10–15 m costs 1.5× the package price, 15–20 m costs 2× (see the table on the prices page). Over 20 m we price individually — send a photo and the length.

How much is interior cleaning on its own?

It depends on how dirty the interior is. Send us a photo and we’ll agree the price upfront. We clean without chemicals.

Do you offer a season subscription?

Yes. We agree on a fixed schedule for the season — it costs less than one-off bookings and you get priority visits.

How much is the trip to Kotor or Tivat?

Tivat is +€15; Kotor, Dobrota and Prčanj are +€20. No supplement in Herceg Novi. For other spots in the bay we tell you the amount upfront.

Can the price change after the job?

No. We fix the price before we start and it doesn’t change afterwards.
